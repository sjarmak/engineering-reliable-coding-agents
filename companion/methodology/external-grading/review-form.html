External evidence-grading calibration

Review procedure

  1. Work independently. Do not consult the monograph catalog, evidence ledger, author labels, or another reviewer.
  2. Grade the support supplied by each cited source for the scoped claim shown here. Do not grade venue prestige or the practice as a whole.
  3. Open the cited source whenever the citation, claim, and boundary are insufficient. When ambiguity remains, choose the lower group and explain why.
  4. Give every item one group and a short rationale, then export the completed JSON response.

StrongOn-claim controlled comparison, validated benchmark result, or comparably specific measurement within stated conditions.

DirectionalSupports a mechanism, threat model, comparison design, or direction without establishing the complete recommendation, magnitude, or broad transfer.

CorroboratingCase report, practitioner account, or convergent observation establishing plausibility without estimating prevalence.

Null or conflictingA result that does not support the expected effect or materially limits another claim.
